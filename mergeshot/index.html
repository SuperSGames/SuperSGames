Aim, shoot and merge!

Launch adorable animals at your chosen angle. Match identical animals to create larger ones, use bounces and rolling motion to trigger chain merges, and manage your space below the danger line as you chase a new high score.

  • 11 animal tiers
  • Angle-based aiming and physics-driven chain merges
  • Bomb, Shake and Shuffle: one use each per game in the Microsoft Store edition
  • No ads, in-app purchases, online leaderboard or revive feature in the Microsoft Store edition.
